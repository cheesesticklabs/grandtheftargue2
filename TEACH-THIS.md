# AI Dilemma Club — six-debate edition (no passwords)

This version contains the six new AI literacy cases, with the first three original debates removed. Every case, the exit ticket, and the save page are accessible directly via navigation. **No passwords or teacher unlock controls** are required. The overall layout and student experience stay the same: initial vote, written argument, revealable twist, second vote, concept, reflection, and PDF/text export.

## The six debates

1. **The AI best friend** — Does a helpful chatbot really understand someone? (Chinese Room)
2. **The fake principal** — Who is responsible for an AI voice hoax? (Synthetic media)
3. **The AI-washing scandal** — Is a useful tutoring app worth buying if it lies about what it does? (AI-washing)
4. **The AI doctor** — A patient dies after an AI misses an illness. Who is responsible? (Accountability)
5. **The car that chose** — How should a self-driving car decide when lives are at risk? (Safety priorities)
6. **The voice after death** — Should an AI recreate a deceased person's voice without consent? (Consent)

## Suggested class structure

- Do Now (3 min)
- Choose any three cases (7 min each). Students vote/write, discuss, reveal twist, and reconsider.
- Exit ticket (4 min)
- Download and submit PDF or text (2 min)

**Twists:** Students still need to type an initial explanation to reveal a twist. That is a writing prompt, not a password. You can jump to any case at any time.

**Hosting:** `index.html` is at the root of this folder. Upload the folder contents to Netlify or GitHub Pages. All JavaScript, CSS, assets, and the PDF library are included locally. Student responses are saved in their own browser but not submitted automatically.
